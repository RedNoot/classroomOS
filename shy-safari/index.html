Your browser will ask to use the microphone. Sound is measured on this computer and never recorded or sent anywhere.

🎬 Music/Movie mode

The microphone switches to echo cancellation, so music or a movie coming out of your speakers is removed before it's measured. Only the class's voices count.

Play it here

Works best when the sound plays in this window. Playing something turns Music/Movie mode on.

Tips
  • Chrome and Edge also cancel sound from their other tabs, so a YouTube or Spotify tab in the same browser works too.
  • Sound from other apps (a desktop music app, a DVD player) can't be fully removed. Play it in the browser instead.
  • Keep the speakers at a normal level. Very loud speakers can leak through.
  • Calibrate once in Music/Movie mode (Settings) for the most accurate goal.
